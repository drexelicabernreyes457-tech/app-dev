# My Favorite Series: The Batman & Movie Universe

Welcome to my repository! Here is some information about one of my favorite movie series.

## 🦇 About the Franchise
* **Genre:** Action, Crime, Drama, Mystery
* **Core Theme:** Justice, Vigilantism, and Resilience

### 🌟 Key Characters
1. **Bruce Wayne / Batman** - The Dark Knight protecting Gotham City.
2. **Alfred Pennyworth** - The loyal butler and mentor.
3. **Jim Gordon** - The honest police commissioner allied with Batman.
